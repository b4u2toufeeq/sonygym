Buying guide

Quick Guide on Choosing the Right Inverter Battery

  1. 1

    Determine Your Power Requirement

    List the appliances you need to run and add up their total wattage.

  2. 2

    Assess the Required Backup Duration

    Decide how many hours of backup you need during a typical outage.

  3. 3

    Check the Battery Voltage

    Match the battery voltage to your inverter's rated input for safe operation.

  4. 4

    Calculate the Correct Battery Capacity

    Size the Ah capacity to your load and backup duration for the best fit. Formula: Ah = (watts × hours) ÷ 12.
