SONY KIDS WORLD - STATIC WEBSITE
================================

TECHNOLOGY
- HTML5
- CSS3
- Bootstrap 5.3.3 (CDN)
- Vanilla JavaScript
- No React, Angular, Node.js, database or build process required.

UPLOAD TO CPANEL
1. Extract sony-kids-world-website.zip on your computer.
2. Open cPanel -> File Manager -> public_html (or your domain's document root).
3. Upload the contents of the sony-kids-world-site folder, not the parent folder itself.
4. Ensure index.html is directly inside public_html.
5. Open your domain and test the navigation, filters and WhatsApp order buttons.

WHATSAPP
The order buttons are configured for +91 93909 89002. The wa.me URL uses the international number without + or spaces.

BRANDING
The Sony Kids World logo crop and two WebP hero banners are based on artwork supplied/generated for this project.

PRODUCT CATALOG
The first version includes four product listings discovered on the referenced 11Cart collection page. Product photos are loaded from 11Cart's public CDN. These external images require an internet connection and may be changed or removed by their host. For a production launch, obtain permission to reuse those product photos and download approved images into assets/images, then update the image URLs in index.html and assets/js/main.js.

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
- Product names, prices and some specifications are based on public 11Cart listings and may change. Verify every price, stock status, specification, delivery promise, warranty and return policy with your supplier before publishing.
- Do not copy 11Cart's company contact details, policies, reviews or claims. Add Sony Kids World's actual business address, support hours, shipping, returns, warranty and privacy information before launch.
- Licensed vehicle brand names and logos may be protected trademarks. Use only where you are authorised to sell/advertise those products.
- This is a WhatsApp enquiry storefront. It does not include online payment, inventory management, customer accounts or an admin panel.
