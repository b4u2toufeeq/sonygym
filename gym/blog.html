What does the Sony Gym blog cover?

The Sony Gym blog is written for people in and around Adilabad, and for anyone passing through on NH 44, about the gym we run on Vidya Nagar in Bhuktapur and the programmes we offer. If you are deciding whether to join a gym at all, or which programme to start with, the answers are here. We write about the things that decide whether you keep training — what a membership actually costs, how to read a training plan, why diet advice fails, and how to start safely at different ages — rather than posting filler. Every article is written by the coaching team, and nothing here is a sponsored post.

Articles are published here as they are finished rather than on a fixed schedule, because we would rather post three that are worth reading than thirty that pad the page. If there is a question you want answered, call us on +91 93909 89002 and we will write it.
