How much does a gym membership cost at Sony Gym?

Sony Gym, on NH 44, Vidya Nagar, Bhuktapur, Adilabad, Telangana 504001, sells membership in three durations — monthly, quarterly and annual — and we share the current rate on a call or WhatsApp rather than printing it on the page, so the figure you are given is the figure you pay. Every tier includes full access to the training floor, all group fitness classes, a locker, and the monthly body composition and weight check.

Personal training and nutrition coaching are sold as add-ons rather than being locked behind one tier. The annual plan is the best value because it includes the full diet programme and its reviews.
