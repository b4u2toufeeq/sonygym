Frequently asked questions

Finding the right guide for your situation.

Book the free assessment first. It is 40 minutes, it includes body composition and a strength baseline, and it identifies anything that is already restricted. From there, most people over 40 either start with Senior Citizen Fitness for a gentler entry point or Strength Foundation if they are comfortable training. We will tell you which, and we will say so if the answer is that you should see a doctor first.

Return from Injury under Training safely. It is a 6-week guided rebuild from reduced range and light load. Bring your physiotherapist's notes to the assessment so the plan is built around them rather than alongside them.

You do not need to work it out on your own — that is what the free assessment is for. Read Choosing what to train to understand the options, then let the coach recommend one from your actual numbers rather than from a guess.
