A gym that plans
the whole programme

Most people do not fail at fitness because of laziness. They fail because nobody told them which lift to add, which weight to use, or how much to eat. At Sony Gym we start with measurements — weight, body fat percentage, waist, and the specific movements that already feel restricted — then build a written plan around them.

From there it is simple: track the numbers you can measure, progress the load only when your form is clean, and adjust the food plan to the results you are getting. Beginners, athletes, weight-loss clients and senior citizens all train in the same hall, on scaled versions of the same programme.
