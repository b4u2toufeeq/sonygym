Fitness programmes at Sony Gym

What fitness programmes does Sony Gym run?

Sony Gym, on NH 44, Vidya Nagar, Bhuktapur, Adilabad, Telangana 504001, runs six structured programmes, each with a fixed length, a named coach and a target agreed at the start: Strength Foundation (8 weeks), Fat Loss Camp (12 weeks), Yoga & Mobility (6 weeks), Sports Conditioning (10 weeks), Senior Citizen Fitness (8 weeks) and Return from Injury (6 weeks). Membership at the gym is separate from the programme, so you can switch tracks without changing how you pay for the floor.

Programmes run in small groups or one-to-one, and the first session of every one of them is a free assessment, so the plan is built from your numbers. To join a programme, call or WhatsApp +91 93909 890002 or send an enquiry.

How a programme runs

Every programme has the same three mechanics, which is what makes the outcome repeatable rather than a matter of who turned up that day.

  • A target agreed at the start. A number and a date, written into the plan — not "get fit".
  • One named coach. The same person runs the sessions and the reviews, so nothing gets lost between shifts.
  • Reviewed every four weeks. Weight, waist, body composition and the strength numbers, then the programme is adjusted.

You keep a copy of the plan, and you are told when the target has been missed — not only when it has been met.
