Who coaches at Sony Gym in Adilabad?

Sony Gym, on NH 44, Vidya Nagar, Bhuktapur, Adilabad, Telangana 504001, is coached by three people rather than whoever happens to be on the floor. Meet the coaching team

Three roles, three specialisms, and no rotating staff on the floor.

Rahul Deshmukh

Strength & conditioning

Runs the strength foundation and sports conditioning programmes, and writes the progressive blocks most members train on after their first few months. Works with cricket, kabaddi, football and athletics athletes on speed, agility, power and return-from-injury progressions.

Sneha Kulkarni

Fat loss & nutrition

Leads the fat loss camp and writes the diet plans. Her plans are built from dal, rice, roti, vegetables and seasonal fruit, budgeted for a normal household, and reviewed every four to six weeks as the numbers move. She runs the monthly body composition checks.

Programs: Fat Loss Camp, Nutrition & Diet Planning

Anil Verma

Yoga & mobility

Takes the morning yoga and mobility classes, the guided breathing sessions, and the senior citizen programme. Also handles the warm-up and mobility work that sits in front of every strength programme, which is where most lower-back problems get sorted out before they become injuries.

Programs: Yoga & Mobility, Senior Citizen

What a coach actually does for you

A coach who is only there to count repetitions is not coaching. Here is what you should expect from any session at Sony Gym, and what you should ask for if you are not getting it.

  • Your programme is written down, and you keep a copy.
  • Every set in your first month is watched, not just counted.
  • Load goes up only when the form is clean and the last session was completed.
  • One coach stays with you, so the same person reviews your numbers each month.
  • If you have an injury or a medical condition, the plan changes before the session starts.
  • Nobody is sold supplements they do not need, and nobody is told to buy a package to keep training.
