Gym services in Adilabad

What services does Sony Gym offer?

Sony Gym, on NH 44, Vidya Nagar, Bhuktapur, Adilabad, Telangana 504001, offers six services: one-to-one personal training, group fitness classes, yoga and meditation, strength and conditioning, body and weight tracking, and nutrition coaching with Indian-food diet plans. Every member starts with a free body composition and BMI assessment, and a written plan is prepared before the first session. You can join a single service or use the full floor — there is no separate premium tier required for coaching.

Not sure which one fits? Call or WhatsApp +91 93909 89002 and describe your goal and how many days a week you can train. We will tell you honestly which service helps and which does not.

Personal training

One coach, one plan, every set supervised. Personal training is the fastest route if you have a specific goal and little time: fat loss, muscle gain, absolute strength, or getting back to training after a long break.

The first session is a movement screen, not a workout. From that the coach builds the programme, and watches every set for the first month until your form is consistent enough for the load to go up. After that most members train on their own with check-ins.

Sessions can be booked as a pack, weekly, or as part of an annual membership.

Group fitness classes

More than 35 classes a week, spread across morning, evening and Sunday slots. HIIT and circuit work, core and abs, functional fitness, strength conditioning, mobility, and dance-based cardio for people who do not want a treadmill.

Classes are capped at a number the coach can actually watch, so you get corrections rather than just music. Beginners are welcome in every class — the coach scalses the session into two difficulty levels and says which one you should be in.

Women-only hours run in the morning, and women-only yoga, core and functional classes are on the weekly schedule.

Yoga & meditation

Hatha yoga and an early-morning mobility flow, aimed at the things that get worse when you sit all day: lower-back tightness, hip mobility, hamstring length and shoulder stiffness.

The guided breathing sessions at the end are the part most members keep coming for. They are useful for blood pressure and for bringing stress down, and they need no equipment and no experience.

The senior citizen programme borrows the same mat-area format with chair-supported versions of every pose.

Strength & conditioning

A progressive strength block first — the main barbell lifts, clean reps, controlled tempo — followed by athletic work for speed, agility, power and injury prevention. This is the route most members take after a few months of basics.

Sports conditioning runs in groups of six to eight, timed around cricket, kabaddi, football, volleyball and athletics schedules, so in-season athletes keep training without wrecking match fitness.

We also work with school and district-level athletes on strength, mobility and return-from-injury progressions.

Body & weight tracking

Weigh-in, waist measurement and body composition every month, recorded on the same machine and at the same time of day so the trend is real rather than noisy.

This is what makes the plan accountable. Without numbers, both the coach and the member are guessing. With them, a stalled plan becomes obvious in four weeks instead of four months — and a plan that is working is obvious too.

Tracking is included with every membership tier. There is no extra charge for the monthly review.

Nutrition & diet planning

The nutrition coach builds the plan around food you already eat at home: dal, rice, roti, vegetables, curd, and seasonal fruit — not imported powders and not a menu nobody in Adilabad can buy.

The plan is budgeted, because a diet that costs more than the member can spend is not a plan. It is reviewed every four to six weeks and adjusted as weight, measurements and training load change.

Included with annual memberships, and available as an add-on with monthly and quarterly plans. Supplements are recommended only where they are genuinely useful.
