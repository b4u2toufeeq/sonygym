Which electric 2-wheelers does Sony Motors sell in Adilabad?

Sony Motors is the electric 2-wheeler arm of Sony Enterprises, opposite Leena Gujrati Hospital, Adilabad, Telangana 504001. We sell eleven scooters — the Sony Glide Pro, Glide Plus, Blossom, Nebo, Nebo FX, Nebo Ola, Nebo Super, Nebo XL, Nebo Advanced and Nebo Plus are all non-RTO, and the Sony Zenith is a registered RTO model. Ten of them run 70 to 90 km on a charge, the Nebo Advanced runs 70 to 85 km, and the Zenith reaches 90 to 120 km. Every model offers lead acid or lithium-ion batteries except the Zenith, which is lithium-ion only, and every scooter is available in up to seven colours.

FAQ

Electric Scooter Questions, Answered

A non-registration-required scooter can be ridden without a registration certificate or a driving licence, where local regulations permit. Ten of the eleven Sony Motors models are non-RTO, which is what makes them popular with students and first-time riders.

The Sony Zenith leads with 90–120 km on its lithium-ion pack. Among the non-RTO models, the Sony Nebo XL is the longest-legged at 80–90 km, followed by the Glide Pro, Glide Plus, Nebo Plus, Blossom and Nebo Super at 75–90 km.

Lead acid is the cheaper upfront option and fine for short daily commutes. Lithium-ion costs more at purchase but is noticeably lighter, holds more usable charge and lasts considerably longer, so it usually works out cheaper over a few years. Both are offered on ten of the eleven models.
