Livfast Inverter Batteries: Backup Your Power Backup

Inverters are key to steady power supply and undisturbed flow of work in the event of power cuts. Backing your power backup with the right batteries is essential to ensure optimal efficiency and longevity. Livfast’s line of power backup solutions not only includes premium-quality inverters but also a selection of relevant, dependable and smart inverter batteries. Be it a state-of-the-art recirculation factory technology to ensure consistent performance or the combination of negative active material with active carbon for 25% extra back up, Livfast batteries have much to offer!

Discover Livfast Inverter Battery Range

Livfast offers a robust range of inverter batteries built with advanced technologies; take a look:

Tall Tubular Batteries

These are taller and narrower batteries with extended backup times. They are perfect for spaces experiencing high power shutdowns and homes of good storage capacity.

Short Tubular Batteries

These inverter batteries are slimmer and shorter in stature and work best where space is limited. They offer efficient performance for spaces facing moderate power cuts.

Short Tubular Jumbo Batteries

These batteries have a compact size and a high backup power capacity. They offer the benefits of both short and tall tubular batteries, meeting diverse power requirements.

Flat Plate Batteries

Flat plate batteries require minimum space and low maintenance while providing power-packed performance. These budget-friendly batteries are best suited for frequent power cuts.

Quick Guide on Choosing the Right Inverter Battery

Livfast has come up with a long list of inverter batteries; don’t get overwhelmed and follow these steps to choose the correct battery for your inverter:

Determine Your Power Requirement

The best inverter battery for your space depends on the power consumption. It is crucial to first evaluate your power requirements.
Suppose you need 60 watts (for 3 tube lights), 210 watts (for 3 fans) and 140 watts (for 2 laptops), which add up to a total power requirement of 410 watts.

Assess The Required Backup Duration

How many hours do you need the power back up for? It plays an important role in the battery requirement.
For now, assume you need a backup for 4 hours.

Check the Battery Voltage

The battery voltage generally is 12 V, 24 V, and 48 V. Read the product description of your inverter to confirm the voltage; it will be mentioned in the capacity section.
Suppose you checked, and the voltage is 12 V

Calculate the Correct Battery Capacity

To calculate the battery capacity, you can use a simple formula – your power requirement * backup duration / Battery Voltage of the inverter.
Considering the numbers we supposed above, the battery capacity you need is: 410* 4 / 12 = 136.6
So, you will need a battery with a capacity of around 136 Ah.

Installation and Maintenance Tips for Inverter Batteries

It doesn’t require much effort or time; here’s how:

  • Install the battery in a safe area, away from children’s reach.
  • Always install the inverter in a well-ventilated area to prevent overheating and minimise the frequent water top-ups.
  • Use the battery regularly to keep it functioning well. If not in use for a long time, discharge the battery once a month and recharge it.
  • Check the water level every 2 to 3 months. It should be between the minimum and maximum limit. Always top with distilled water. Rainwater or tap water can affect durability and performance as they carry excess minerals and impurities.
  • Clean the surface and sides of the battery and keep it dust-free.
  • Keep the battery terminals corrosion or rust-free, as buildup can disrupt current flow, slow down charging, and shorten battery life.
  • Ensure there are no blocked vents, as it will allow hydrogen gas accumulation, and the battery may burst.

How do I know if my inverter battery needs replacement?

If the battery is damaged or dead, replace it. You can stay updated about the battery condition through regular checks.

Which inverter battery is better, tubular or flat?

Tubular batteries provide a longer life and can sustain heavy loads. In contrast, flat-plate batteries are cost-effective and more suitable for areas with frequent power cuts.

Is this Helpful?

How long does an inverter battery last?

It varies depending on the battery. For example, a tubular battery lasts approximately 5 to 6 years, while that of a flat plate battery is between 3 and 4 years.

Is this Helpful?

How do you increase the life of an inverter battery?

You can increase the lifespan of an inverter battery by:

    • Properly installing it in a well-ventilated area
    • Ensuring regular maintenance
    • Keeping the battery charged
    • Avoiding overcharging and deep draining

What is the lifespan of an inverter battery?

The lifespan of an inverter battery typically ranges from 3 to 5 years, depending on factors like usage, maintenance, and environmental conditions. Proper maintenance can extend its life.

How long does it take to charge an inverter battery?

With Livfast batteries, you can enjoy up to 25% faster charging, allowing for quicker recovery during power outages. The charging time varies depending on battery size and capacity.

Can I use a different brand’s battery with my Livfast inverter?

For optimal performance, it’s recommended to use Livfast batteries with Livfast inverters. While other batteries may work, compatibility issues may arise, affecting performance and longevity.

How do I know if my inverter battery is low?

Livfast inverters and batteries are equipped with a low charge indicator, providing a clear visual of your battery’s remaining charge. You will be notified when it’s time to recharge.
